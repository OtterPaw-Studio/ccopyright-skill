# Official page-level source catalog

Last manually reviewed: 2026-09-01

Use this catalog to reach an official page that directly supports an answer.
It is not a website mirror and does not make page content permanently current.
The China Copyright Protection Center service pages below showed no clear page
publication or update date during review, so the catalog records the actual
access date only and does not treat HTTP metadata as a content date.

## Official legislation and rules

| Source | Useful for | Currency boundary |
|---|---|---|
| National People's Congress: [Copyright Law of the People's Republic of China](https://www.npc.gov.cn/c2/c30834/202011/t20201119_308796.html) | Higher-level copyright framework | Check for later amendments |
| National administrative-regulation database: [consolidated Regulations on Computer Software Protection, including the 2011 and 2013 revisions](https://xzfg.moj.gov.cn/mobile/law/detail?LawID=581) | Software-protection and ownership framework | Complex ownership still needs specialist judgment |
| National Copyright Administration: [Measures for Computer Software Copyright Registration](https://www.ncac.gov.cn/xxfb/flfg/bmgz/202410/t20241015_869486.html) | Applicants, main materials, identification material, ordinary/exceptional deposit, proof categories, and A4 | Main ordinary-registration rule baseline |

These sources support the institutional and material framework. They do not by
themselves establish today's portal fields, upload limits, certificate
arrangements, or displayed processing behavior.

## Choosing the basis

- For legal or material rules, start with legislation, regulations, or the
  registration measures.
- For how the official service currently operates, use the matching service
  page and the applicant's current authenticated portal.
- For field meaning, use the form instructions and then verify the current form
  wording.
- For time, fee, or certificate delivery, use a current official page and state
  the access date.
- For a third-party package, official pages separate official work from the
  package; the provider's own current redacted terms establish its scope and
  price.

## Relationship to the portal validation profile

The fields, conditional branches, and character gates in
`ccopyright-register` are a portal-compatibility profile, not an official
source in this catalog. QA may explain what that bundled profile checks, but
must label the value as locally configured and subject to current-portal
verification. The profile alone cannot establish a current official rule.
