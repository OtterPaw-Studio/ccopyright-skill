# Source, currency, and citation policy

The legal text is relatively stable, but portal fields, upload constraints,
channels, fees, displayed timelines, and third-party offerings can change. Do
not answer those questions from model memory.

## Source priority

Choose the source that directly supports the kind of claim rather than applying
one mechanical order to every question:

1. legal, rights, and material baselines: current official legislation,
   regulations, and rules;
2. service steps, file instructions, and common questions: the specific China
   Copyright Protection Center guide or FAQ page;
3. dynamic fields for the applicant's current operation: authenticated current
   portal text or current redacted content explicitly supplied for this
   question;
4. third-party sources only for that third party's own offering or as leads,
   never as sole proof of an official requirement.

Start with the [official page-level source catalog](official-sources.md). The
bundled portal validation profile is not a source class.

## Currency classes

### Verify a current source every time

- official fee items or amounts;
- current channel, account requirement, and operating steps;
- form fields, choices, help text, and character limits;
- PDF size, naming, orientation, combination, and upload count;
- displayed processing timeline and electronic/paper certificate arrangements;
- provider package, price, timeline, refund, or “expedited” claims.

### An official rule baseline can be used with a boundary statement

- main categories of application material stated by the rule;
- ordinary program/document identification-material structure;
- categories of conditional proof;
- Chinese-language and A4 baselines in the rule.

## Citation record

For a changeable conclusion, include when practical:

- the page or document title;
- a direct link;
- the actual access or current-portal review date;
- the section supporting the conclusion;
- what the source still does not establish.

A search snippet can locate a page. Return to the official page or current text
explicitly supplied for this question before treating the claim as established.
Do not relabel attachments previously viewed to design the Skill as Q&A
evidence.

## Portal validation profile

The `ccopyright-register` fields, branches, character minimums/maximums, and
unknowns are an updateable compatibility profile. They produce draft warnings
and final gates; they do not establish current official requirements. When
explaining a configured value, state the profile ID, its non-authoritative
status, and the need for current-portal verification. If a current official
page or portal conflicts, use the current information for the application and
update the register workspace configuration.

## Third-party sources

- A provider page can establish what that provider displayed on the access
  date; it cannot establish a market price or an official necessity.
- A user-supplied quote supports analysis of that quote only.
- Comparing current prices across providers is a separate research task; do not
  start it unless the user explicitly asks.
- Do not promote forum, video, or marketing experience into a permanent rule.

## Conflict and unavailable access

When a current official page or portal conflicts with the bundled validation
profile, use the information the applicant reviewed for this application and
explain the conflict. If an official page cannot be accessed, state that the
current value is unverified; do not silently substitute a configured value.
